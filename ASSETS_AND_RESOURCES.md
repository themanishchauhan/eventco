## 🎯 Usage Instructions
* **Photos**: Use high-resolution `.jpg` or `.webp` images from these folders to populate the Astro content collection in `src/content/gallery/{weddings, corporate, decor, cultural}/`.
* **Video Clips**: 
  * **Hero Background**: Short, compressed 5–10 second looping ambient video clips (`.mp4` / `.webm`) muted with `autoplay loop playsinline`.
  * **Gallery Lightbox**: Video reels/clips can be played directly inside the `glightbox` modal viewer.
* **Logos**: Extract SVG/vector logo assets for header/footer placement.
